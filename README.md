# YouTube Income per 1000 Views Calculator

**Live site:** https://yourusername.github.io/

A free tool that estimates how much YouTube pays per 1,000 views (RPM). Choose your niche, audience country and video type to see a realistic earnings range in US dollars or Indian rupees. No signup, no tracking of what you enter.

## What you can do

- Enter any number of views and see the estimated earnings and a low-to-high range
- Compare **long videos** and **YouTube Shorts**
- See how income changes by **niche** (finance, tech, gaming, music and more)
- See how income changes by **audience country** (US, UK, India, Brazil and more)
- Switch between **USD and INR** with an editable exchange rate
- Find out how many views you need to earn $1,000 or ₹1 lakh

## How the estimate works

```
Earnings = (Views ÷ 1,000) × RPM
```

RPM is what a creator earns per 1,000 views after YouTube's share is removed. The calculator starts from a typical US range for your niche, then multiplies it by a country factor (for example, India is about 0.10 of the US rate). Shorts use a separate, much lower range.

| Format | Typical RPM (US audience) |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Long video | $0.50 to $30, depending on niche |
| Shorts | $0.02 to $0.08 |

## Accuracy

The numbers are **estimates** based on commonly reported creator figures. They are not official YouTube data and are not a guarantee of income. Your real earnings depend on audience, season, watch time and advertiser demand. For exact figures, check **YouTube Studio > Analytics > Revenue**.
